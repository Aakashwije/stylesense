# stylesense
StyleSens is a modern smart salon platform designed to connect customers with salons through one centralized and user-friendly experience. **The platform allows users to explore multiple salons in one place, view available services, compare ratings, and book appointments easily online.**

StyleSens also enhances the beauty experience with intelligent features such as **AI-powered recommendations, virtual hairstyle previews, chatbot assistance, real-time notifications, and secure online payments. **Customers can manage their bookings through a personalized dashboard, while salon owners can manage appointments, services, and business activities through a dedicated salon dashboard.

The goal of StyleSens is to simplify salon discovery and appointment management while creating a more interactive, personalized, and technology-driven beauty experience.
